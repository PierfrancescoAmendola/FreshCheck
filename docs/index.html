FreshCheck

Track food dates, waste less

FreshCheck tells you what's in your fridge and what needs eating first. Scan a barcode, read the printed date with your camera, get a reminder before anything goes off, and cook recipes with what's ending. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no servers, no tracking.
